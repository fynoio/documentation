Before we get started, you will need to have a Gupshup Enterprise account already set up, along with SenderID and Templates.

<Steps>
### Find the Provider
Navigate to the Integrations page. Under "RCS" section, click on the "MSG91" button.

### Configure the Integration

In the pop-up, fill in:

- **Custom name**: Provide a name that would help you identify the configured account in Fyno's portal.
- **API Key**: Log in to your MSG91 RCS account, navigate to the Authkey settings, and copy the Authkey
- **Project ID**: Fetch Project ID from your account settings.

### Complete the Integration

Click on "Add Account" once done, and you are all set!

</Steps>

## Message Delivery Status

While Fyno has the capacity to track the notification delivery status, MSG91 requires a manual update of the Fyno Callback Endpoint in order to receive these reports. To update the Callback manually follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step>Copy the Callback URL in the Integrations popup.</Step>
  <Step>Send the Callback URL to your MSG91 account manager to add it in your MSG91 account.</Step>
</Steps>
