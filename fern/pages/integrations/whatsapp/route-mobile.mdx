Before we get started, you will need an active WhatsApp number as well as templates pre-configured in your Route Mobile account

<Steps>
### Find the Provider
Navigate to the Integrations page. Under "WhatsApp" section, click on the "Route Mobile" button.

### Configure the Integration

In the pop-up that appears, fill in:

- **Custom name:** Provide a name that would help you identify the configured account in Fyno's portal.
- **Username:** Provide the username that you use to sign into your Route Mobile Account.
- **Password:** Provide the password that you use to sign in, corresponding to the same Route Mobile account

### Complete the Integration

Click on "Add Account" once done, and you are all set!

</Steps>

## Message Delivery Status

While Fyno has the capacity to track the notification delivery status, Route Mobile requires a manual update of the Fyno Callback Endpoint in order to receive these reports. To update the Callback manually follow these steps:

<Steps>

<Step>Log in to your Route Mobile account.</Step>

<Step>Navigate to the **Profile Management** section within your WhatsApp settings.</Step>

<Step>Enter Delivery callback URL (you will get it from Route Mobile integrations popup) into the designated **Callback URL** field.</Step>

</Steps>
