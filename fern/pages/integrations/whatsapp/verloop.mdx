Before we get started, you will need an active WhatsApp number as well as templates pre-configured in your Verloop account

<Steps>
### Find the Provider
Navigate to the Integrations page. Under "WhatsApp" section, click on the "Verloop" button.

### Configure the Integration

In the pop-up that appears, fill in:

- **Custom name:** Provide a name to help you identify the configured account in Fyno's portal.
- **Client ID:** In your Verloop Account, you will get the Client ID
- **Access Token:** In your Verloop Account, you will get the Access Token.

### Complete the Integration

Click on "Add Account" once done, and you are all set!

</Steps>

## Message Delivery Status

Verloop, by default, provides the status of the message without any additional set-up needed.

Once the above integration is complete, the status of the notification, which will be shared by Verloop will be displayed in the logs, when you click on it, under the **[Delivery](./log-details-delivery)** tab.
