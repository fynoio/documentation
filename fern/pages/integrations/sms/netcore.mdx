Before we get started, you will need to have a Netcore account already set up, along with SenderID and Templates.

<Steps>

### Find the Provider

Navigate to the Integrations page. Under “SMS” section, click on the Netcore button.

### Configure the Integration

In the pop-up, fill in:

- **Custom name**: Provide a name that would help you identify the configured account in Fyno's portal.

- **Account ID**: This is the unique identifier representing the channel owned by the enterprise.  

- **Username**: The CustomerID that is displayed on your Netcore Account when you log in and view the dashboard

- **Password**: Password used to log into the Customer ID entered in the previous field

- **Sender**: The SenderID that is being used by you to send out messages.

### Complete the Integration

Click on "Add Account" once done, and you are all set!

</Steps>

## Message Delivery Status

Netcore, by default, provides the status of the message without any additional setup needed.

Once the above integration is complete, the status of the notification, which will be shared by Netcore will be displayed in the logs, when you click on it, under the **[Delivery](./log-details-delivery)** tab.
