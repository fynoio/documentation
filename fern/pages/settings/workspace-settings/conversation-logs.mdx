Conversation Logs enables you to view all the customer replies received via SMS and WhatsApp.

To enable it,

<Steps>
  <Step>Go to Workspace Settings.</Step>
  <Step>Select Add-Ons.</Step>
  <Step>Enable the toggle in Conversation Logs.</Step>
</Steps>

Once its enabled, you will be able to view all the responses sent by your customers under Logs -> Inbound. You can read about Inbound logs [here](./inbound-logs).
