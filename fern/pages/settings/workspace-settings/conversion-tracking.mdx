Conversation Tracking enables you to track how many users did the desired action after sending the notification.

To enable it,

<Steps>
  <Step>Go to Workspace Settings.</Step>
  <Step>Select Add Ons.</Step>
  <Step>Enable the toggle in Conversation Tracking.</Step>
</Steps>

Once its enabled, you will be able to see the Conversion tracking option in notification event page. You can read about it [here](./notification-event-creation#track-conversion-optional).
