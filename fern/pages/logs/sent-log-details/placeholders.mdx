On the "Log Details" pop-up, the second tab on the ribbon menu you will see is "Placeholders".

Placeholders shows you the details of all the placeholders or the replaceable parameters that were in the "Template" that was used to trigger the notification event.

<Info title="No details in Placeholders?">If the "Placeholders" tab shows no details, this only means that the content that was triggered in the notification had no replaceable parameters to be displayed.</Info>

<Note> If any placeholders or base64 attachment size is greater than 1 MB, then you will see 'content omitted due to size' as we don't store any content > 1 MB at our end.  </Note>
