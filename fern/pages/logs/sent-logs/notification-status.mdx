This document explains the different notification statuses, tracking the lifecycle from initiation on the Client's side to final delivery to the end customer.

## API Request Processing Status

These statuses are generated immediately after API requests are received from the Client and processed by Fyno.

| Status | Description | Email | WhatsApp | SMS | RCS | InApp | Push | WebPush | Voice | Slack/Discord/Teams |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Error** | Message failed at Fyno's end | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |
| **Failed** | Message failed at Provider's end | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |
| **Timeout** | Provider failed to respond within 5–10 seconds | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |
| **Dropped** | Message was dropped due to Fyno configurations (suppression/route/subscription) | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |
| **Attempted** | Message delivery was attempted and routed to the provider | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |

## Submission Status

These statuses are recorded after Fyno successfully hands over the request to the external service provider.

| Status | Description | Email | WhatsApp | SMS | RCS | InApp | Push | WebPush | Voice | Slack/Discord/Teams |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Queued** | Message was queued at the provider's end | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **Sent / Processed** | Message request was processed and sent out for final delivery | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |

---

## Undelivered & Unconfirmed Notification Status

These statuses occur when a notification is sent out by the service provider but fails to reach the recipient, or fails to return a delivery receipt.

| Status | Description | Email | WhatsApp | SMS | RCS | InApp | Push | WebPush | Voice | Slack/Discord/Teams |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **INVALID-SUB** | Subscriber/recipient details were invalid | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **REJECTED** | Message was rejected by the provider (DLT/Template/Complaint issues) | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **BOUNCE** | Email was bounced back by the receiving server | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **DENIED** | Notification was explicitly denied (Push disabled / Call declined) | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> |
| **FAILED** | Message failed at the provider level (congestion/system error/template mismatch) | <No /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> |
| **NOANSWER** | Voice call went unanswered or the user line was busy | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> |
| **UNDELIVERED** | Message could not be delivered successfully by the provider | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **No DLR** | No Delivery Receipt was returned by the provider network | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |

---

## Successful Delivery & Engagement Status

These statuses track successful notification drop-offs and subsequent user interactions.

| Status | Description | Email | WhatsApp | SMS | RCS | InApp | Push | WebPush | Voice | Slack/Discord/Teams |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **DELIVERED** | Message was delivered successfully to the destination device | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **UPDATED** | Message content was dynamically updated successfully | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **CLICKED** | An embedded URL/Link or the notification action was clicked by the user | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <Yes /> |
| **OPEN** | Message container was opened by the user | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **READ** | Message was marked or flagged as read by the user | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **ANSWERED** | Outbound voice call was successfully answered by the recipient | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> |
| **DELETED** | Notification was manually dismissed or deleted by the user | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **KEYPRESS** | Recipient answered the call and interacted via an IVR keypad entry | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> |
| **REPLIED** | Message received an inbound response/reply from the end-user | <No /> | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **UNSUBSCRIBED** | Recipient chose to opt-out or unsubscribe from this notification group | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |
| **SPAMREPORT** | Recipient manually flagged the incoming email message as SPAM | <Yes /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> | <No /> |
| **UNKNOWN** | Status payload unresolved. (Please flag and report these instances to Fyno) | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> | <Yes /> |
