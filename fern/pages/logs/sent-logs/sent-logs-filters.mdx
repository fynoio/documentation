## Converted & Verified Statuses

Downstream conversion statuses apply globally across channels when activation metrics or security validation systems are turned on.

### Converted

Notification status transitions to **Converted** if Conversion Tracking is enabled for an event and a downstream client action triggers Fyno's conversion analytics payload endpoint.

* **Supported Channels:** Email <Yes /> | WhatsApp <Yes /> | SMS <Yes /> | RCS <Yes /> | InApp <Yes /> | Push <Yes /> | WebPush <Yes /> | Voice <Yes /> | Slack/Discord/Teams <Yes />

### Verified

Notification status transitions to **Verified** when a transaction involves a secure OTP code or unique security links successfully resolved via Fyno Verify workflows.

* **Supported Channels:** Email <Yes /> | WhatsApp <Yes /> | SMS <Yes /> | RCS <Yes /> | InApp <Yes /> | Push <Yes /> | WebPush <Yes /> | Voice <Yes /> | Slack/Discord/Teams <Yes />
