Connect a Kafka topic to Fyno Connect with your broker details and authentication settings. Once connected, Fyno consumes the messages on the topic and triggers the workflow you specify for each message.

## Connect your Kafka topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Kafka from Databases">
    Go to **Databases** and click **Kafka**. The **Add a Kafka database** pop-up opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the connection details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    - **Custom Name**: A name to identify this connection in Fyno Connect.
    - **Kafka Brokers**: The addresses of your Kafka brokers in `host:port` format. Separate multiple brokers with commas.
    - **Kafka Topic**: The topic Fyno consumes messages from.
    - **Use SSL**: Enter `true` if your brokers require an SSL connection, otherwise `false`.
    - **SASL**: Your SASL authentication settings as a JSON object, including the mechanism, username and password. Leave this blank if your brokers do not use SASL.

      ```json
      {
        "mechanism": "plain",
        "username": "your-username",
        "password": "your-password"
      }
      ```

    - **Fyno Workflow Endpoint**: The endpoint of the Fyno workflow to trigger for each message.
    - **Automation ID**: The ID of the workflow to trigger.
    - **API Key**: The Fyno API key used to call the workflow.

    All fields except Custom Name are masked by default. Click the eye icon on a field to view its value.

    ![Add a Kafka database pop-up](/images/fyno-connect-kafka-add-database.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the database">
    Click **Add Database**. Fyno tests the connection and saves it once the test succeeds. The connection then appears under **Connected DBs** on the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger the workflow">
    Fyno now consumes messages from the topic and triggers the workflow you specified for each message, passing the message as the workflow input.
  </Step>
</Steps>
