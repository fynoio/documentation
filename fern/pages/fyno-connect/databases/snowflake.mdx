Connect your Snowflake database to Fyno Connect with your account details and credentials. Once connected, you can build cohorts directly from the data in your database.

## Connect your Snowflake database

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Snowflake from Databases">
    Go to **Databases** and click **Snowflake**. The **Add a Snowflake database** pop-up opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the connection details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    - **Custom Name**: A unique name to identify this Snowflake connection in Fyno Connect.
    - **Account Name**: The name of your Snowflake account. To find it, see Snowflake's [account identifier guide](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/admin-account-identifier).
    - **User Name**: The username used to access your Snowflake account.
    - **Password**: The password for that user.
    - **Warehouse**: The name of the warehouse to use.
    - **Database**: The name of the database you want to connect.

    All fields except Custom Name are masked by default. Click the eye icon on a field to view its value.

    ![Add a Snowflake database pop-up](/images/fyno-connect-snowflake-add-database.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the database">
    Click **Add Database**. Fyno tests the connection and saves it once the test succeeds. The database then appears under **Connected DBs** on the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create cohorts">
    You can now create cohorts from the data in your database. See [Create Cohorts](./how-to-create-a-cohort-in-fyno-connect).
  </Step>
</Steps>
