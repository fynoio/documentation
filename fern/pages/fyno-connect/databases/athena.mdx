To connect Fyno Connect with your Athena Database:

<Steps>
  <Step>Navigate to **Databases** and click on Athena.</Step>
  <Step>
    In the pop-up, enter the following details:
    <ul>
      <li>Custom Name</li>
      <li>AWS Region</li>
      <li>AWS Access Key id</li>
      <li>AWS Secret Access Key</li>
      <li>Database</li>
      <li>Output bucket</li>
      <li>Partition Column (Optional)</li>
      <li>Partition Column Value(Optional)</li>
    </ul>
  </Step>
  <Step>Click on "Add Database" to test the connection and successfully save it. After the database is added successfully, you will see it listed on the same page under **Connected DBs**.</Step>
  <Step>You can now create cohorts from the data in your database. See [Create Cohorts.](./how-to-create-a-cohort-in-fyno-connect)</Step>
</Steps>
