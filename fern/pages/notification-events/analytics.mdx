**Notification Event Analytics** shows how a notification event is performing, with all its charts in one place. Use it to understand what is working and to improve the results of that notification event.

To see analytics, you first need a notification event. Once the notification event is created and in use, the notifications it sends are summarised here.

## Open Notification Event Analytics

You can open Notification Event Analytics in 2 ways:

- On the **Notification Events** page, click the analytics icon (bar chart) on the notification event card.
- Open a notification event and click the analytics icon (bar chart) at the top right.

The analytics page opens with the name of the notification event at the top, shown as **Analytics: &lt;notification event name&gt;**. Click the icon next to the name to open the notification event, or click **Notification-Events** at the top left to go back to the list.

![Notification Event Analytics overview](/images/analytics-overview.png)

![A quick look through the analytics page](/images/analytics-page-tour.gif)

## Filters and page options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Time filter** (top left) | Choose the time period to view. The selected date range appears next to it. The filter applies to the whole page. |
| **Analytics Label** (top right) | View analytics for a Dynamic Analytics Label set in your notification event. See [Analytics Label](#analytics-label). |
| **last updated** (top right) | Shows when the data was last refreshed. |
| **Status Guide** | Opens a guide to what each status means. |

The time filter options are:

1. Today
2. Yesterday
3. Last 7 Days
4. Last 28 Days
5. Custom (lets you pick a time range within the last 3 months)

## Overview

The overview cards give you a quick summary of how the notification event is doing.

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Events Fired** | The number of times the notification event was fired, split into **Delivered**, **Undelivered** and **Dropped**. |
| **Notifications Requested** | The number of notification requests sent to Fyno for this notification event. |
| **Notifications Sent** | The number of notifications Fyno sent to your service providers for this notification event. Click **See how?** to learn how it is counted. |
| **Notifications Delivered** | The number of notifications delivered, with the delivery rate in brackets. Click **view split** to see the breakdown. |
| **Converted** | The number of conversions for this notification event, if Track Conversion is turned on. Read more in [Track Conversion](./notification-event-creation#track-conversion-optional). |

<Info title="More notifications than events fired?">That's completely normal! It depends on how many channels and providers are set up in the route or notification event. If you have more than 1 channel or provider, each event can send several notifications, so the count of notifications can be much higher than the number of times the notification event was fired.</Info>

## Sent by Channels

The **Sent by Channels** chart shows how your notifications are split across the channels used. The total number of notifications is shown in the middle, with the count for each channel next to the chart.

## Analytics Label

The **Analytics Label** dropdown at the top right lets you view analytics for a Dynamic Analytics Label set in your notification event. Read more in [Analytics Label](./notification-event-creation#analytics-label-optional).

## Channel Engagement

**Channel Engagement** shows each channel used to send notifications and what happened to them, like a funnel.

![Channel Engagement](/images/analytics-channel-engagement.png)

Use the buttons at the top right of the card to switch between counts (**#**), percentages (**%**) and a table view.

The statuses you may see are:

- **Attempted**: The notification was sent from Fyno.
- **Delivered**: The notification reached the destination (mobile number, email address, or device token in the case of Push).
- **Read/Open**: Read applies only to WhatsApp and In-App notifications. When these notifications are opened or interacted with, the provider returns the status "Read". Open applies only to email. When an email is opened, it is marked as "Open" and tracked in Fyno.
- **Clicked**: The notification was clicked. This applies to In-App, Push, emails with links, and so on.
- **Deleted**: A Push notification was shown on the user's device and the user swiped it away.
- **Keypress**: The user received a voice (IVR) call and pressed a key to continue.
- **Replied**: The user replied to a WhatsApp notification they received.

Read about all the notification statuses for each channel in [Notification Status](./notification-status).

Hover over a bar to see its count.

<Tip>Want to focus on one status? Click the other statuses in the legend below the chart to hide them.</Tip>

## Delivery Distribution

This chart shows which channels, or combinations of channels, your notifications were delivered on. Each bar stands for a channel or a set of channels, shown by their icons. Notifications that were not delivered on any channel appear as **Undelivered**.

![Delivery Distribution](/images/analytics-delivery-distribution.png)

If your notifications are rarely delivered on more than one channel, you'll see a **Kudos to you!** message.

If you use an Omni-Channel Route but still see many notifications delivered on several channels, you can either increase the wait time in your route, or check with your service providers whether they are slow to send delivery statuses to Fyno.

If the notification event uses only one channel, the chart is empty and suggests going [omni-channel](./omnichannel-routing-introduction) for better deliverability.

![Delivery Distribution with a single channel](/images/analytics-delivery-distribution-single.png)

## Delivery Status

The **Delivery Status** chart shows the delivery statuses over time for one channel. To change the channel, click the chart title (for example, **Email Delivery Status**) and pick another channel from the dropdown. Channels are listed by how much they are used.

![Email Delivery Status](/images/analytics-delivery-status-email.png)

- Hover over any point on the chart to see the count and percentage of each status for that day.
- Use the **#** and **%** buttons at the top right to switch between counts and percentages.
- Use the zoom buttons at the bottom right to zoom in and out.
- Click a status in the legend below the chart to show or hide it.

![Hovering over the WhatsApp Delivery Status chart](/images/analytics-delivery-status-hover.gif)

Here are some of the statuses you may see:

- **Requested**: Notifications requested for that channel.
- **Sent**: Notifications that left Fyno successfully towards the destination. If the provider doesn't send a delivery confirmation, the status stays as "Sent".
- **Delivered**: Notifications that reached the destination successfully.
- **Error**: Notifications that could not be sent from Fyno because of an error, for example a problem with the template or no provider selected for a channel.
- **Failed**: Notifications that failed, most likely because a condition required by the provider was not met.
- **Bounce**: Seen only for Email. This happens when:
  - The email address is incorrect.
  - The email address only accepts emails from senders allowlisted on their domain.
  - The mailbox is full and can't receive any more emails.

<Info>
  These statuses are counted as **Undelivered**:
  <ul>
    <li>Invalid-Subscriber</li>
    <li>Blacklist</li>
    <li>Rejected</li>
    <li>Bounce</li>
  </ul>
</Info>

## URL Engagement

**URL Engagement** shows click metrics for the top five shortened URLs in your notifications. Each bar is one shortened URL, and the legend below the chart shows which URL each bar belongs to.
