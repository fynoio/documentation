A notification event is the combination of templates, service providers or route (single or omni-channel).

**Notification Events = Template + (Providers or Route) **

## What does it do?

A notification event is what sends your configured message to the end user.

## How do they work?

<Steps>
  <Step>Add the recipient number or email ID - depending on your use case.</Step>
  <Step>
    Add variables that have been created within the template.
    <ul>
      <li>Variables are usually denoted within **`{{}}`**.</li>
      <li>Each variable needs to be filled out without which null (empty) value will be delivered in place of the placeholder in the message.</li>
    </ul>
  </Step>
</Steps>
