"Omnichannel" means all channels, and that is exactly what an Omnichannel Route gives you: the freedom to mix and match multiple **channels** and **providers** in a single route, from simple fallbacks to flows with several branches and conditions.

## What is an Omnichannel Route?

An Omnichannel Route lets you work with multiple notification channels, including SMS, WhatsApp, Email, Slack, Discord, Teams, Push, InApp, Voice and RCS. You can connect channels:

- **In sequence**, one after another, so they work in tandem.
- **As a failover**, so that a channel acts as a backup for the one before it.

<Tip title="What is the difference between a Single Channel Route and an Omnichannel Route?">
  A Single Channel Route makes connections **within** a channel, between its providers. An Omnichannel Route makes connections **between** channels.
</Tip>

## Why use an Omnichannel Route?

An Omnichannel Route lets you use any channel available to you, at any point in the flow, to make sure your communication actually reaches your customers.

- You are no longer limited to a single channel.
- You no longer have to wonder whether your communication reached your customers.

## When to use an Omnichannel Route?

Used well, an Omnichannel Route solves use cases that would otherwise need coordination across multiple teams and many lines of code.

Take an example. Company ABC has customers in India and the USA, and wants to:

1. Notify customers in India via **SMS**, since it is cheaper and they already have it set up.
2. Notify customers in the USA via **WhatsApp**, since it is their preferred channel.
3. In both countries, wait for a minute after the first notification goes out, and then send a **Push** notification.

Done by hand, this would be time-consuming and need channel-wise coordination across teams. With an Omnichannel Route, you can build it in minutes.

## Manage your routes

On the **Routes → Omni-Channel** page, click the **three dots** icon on a route to open its menu:

- **Rename**: Change the route's name.
- **Copy to another workspace**: Copy the route to other workspaces you own. See [Copy routes to another workspace](./routes#copy-routes-to-another-workspace).
- **View Details**: See where the route is used and what it depends on.
- **Duplicate**: Create a copy of the route in the same workspace.
- **Delete**: Delete the route.
- **Go Live**: Make the route live, as you would from the route builder.

![](/images/route-options-menu.png)

### View details

**View Details** shows when the route was last updated, along with:

- **Used in**: The notification events that use the route.
- **Depends On**: The integrations the route uses.

Use the **Test** and **Live** toggle at the top to see the details for each version.

![](/images/view-route-details.png)

### Duplicate a route

<Steps>
  <Step>Click the **three dots** icon on the route and select **Duplicate**.</Step>
  <Step>
    In the **Duplicate Omni-channel route** dialog, the **Name** is filled in as the route's name followed by `-copy`. Change it if needed, add an optional **Description**, and click **Submit**.

    ![](/images/duplicate-omni-channel-route-dialog.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>
