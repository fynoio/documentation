Creating a Single Channel Route is easy once you have a clear idea of what you want to achieve.

## The Single Channel Routes page

Go to **Routes → Single Channel** in the left navigation. This page lists your existing single channel routes, with when each was last edited. At the top of the page, you can:

- Search for a route by name.
- Filter routes by **Channel**.
- Filter routes by **Version**, for example to see only live routes.
- Switch between a card grid and a list view, using the icon next to **+ Create**.

![](/images/single-channel-routes-listing.png)

## Create a route

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **+ Create** at the top right of the page.</Step>
  <Step>
    In the **Create Single Channel Route** dialog, select the channel from the dropdown. You can create a route for SMS, RCS, WhatsApp, Email, Viber, Slack, Discord, Teams, Push, Voice and InApp.

    ![](/images/single-channel-route-channel-list.png)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter a **Name** for the route. The name starts with the channel you selected, for example `sms-`. Optionally, add a **Description**, then click **Submit**.

    ![](/images/create-single-channel-route-dialog.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Flow builder

Creating a route opens the flow builder, where you design the routing for the channel, including redundancy and failover.

![](/images/single-channel-route-builder.png)

The flow builder has:

- **Route name and version**: At the top left, with the version dropdown showing the current version, for example `v1 (Test)`.
- **Canvas controls**: On the left, to zoom in, zoom out, fit the flow to the screen and view it full screen.
- **Start**: Where every route begins. Below it, **Pick a component to start** lets you add a **Condition** or an **Action**.
- **Stop**: Added after an Action, where that branch of the flow ends.
- **Save** and **Go Live**: At the top right. See [Test and live versions](#test-and-live-versions).

Click a component on the canvas to open its settings panel. There are two components:

- **[Condition](./routing-condition)**: Controls the flow of notifications, so that specific actions run depending on whether the condition is met. Each condition has a **True** and a **False** outcome.
- **[Action](./routing-action)**: Decides how the notification is sent through your providers, using Simply Send, Round Robin, Distribute Between or Do Nothing. Actions also have a **Failover** section, where you add backup providers in case sending fails, helping you keep your "no downtime" promise to your customers.

In the route below, a Condition checks whether the variable `type` equals `ivr`. On **True**, the notification is sent with Simply Send through one provider. On **False**, it is sent with Simply Send through another. Each branch then ends at **Stop**.

## Test and live versions

Every route you create is saved as a **Test** version, so you can try out different variations and combinations to suit your needs. Saving a Test version does not affect the **Live** version, which may be plugged into your application and active.

<Steps>
  <Step>Make your final changes to the route and test it to make sure you get the results you want.</Step>
  <Step>Check the version dropdown next to the route name to see which version you are working on.</Step>
  <Step>To keep the version as a test, click **Save**. The draft is saved in **Test** and is not visible in **Live**.</Step>
  <Step>
    To make the version live, click **Go Live**, then click **Confirm** in the **Go Live Confirmation** dialog. The current version becomes the live version of the route.

    <Note>
      If approvals are turned on for your workspace, clicking **Go Live** asks you to request approval instead. See [Approval Requests](./approval-request) for how this works.
    </Note>

    ![](/images/single-channel-request-approval.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Once a Single Channel Route is live, every notification event it is plugged into automatically uses the live version.</Tip>
