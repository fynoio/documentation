TRAI uses Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), a blockchain-based system, to monitor unsolicited SMS. Implemented in April 2021 as part of the TCCCP Regulations, it requires all SMS sent by Principal Entities (PEs) to be registered on the DLT platform.

## SMS template types

There are 3 types of templates, each with specific content template guidelines.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Transactional" icon="shield-check">
    OTPs and critical, time-sensitive messages. Available only to banks and RBI-approved digital wallets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Service Implicit" icon="bell">
    Non-promotional messages where consent is inferred from the customer's actions or relationship with you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Promotional" icon="megaphone">
    Marketing messages and offers. Require explicit customer consent and consent scrubbing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Transactional">
    Transactional templates are used for:

    - Transaction-related messages (for example, order confirmations and account alerts)
    - Messages with One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for banking transactions
    - Essential and time-sensitive communications

    These templates ensure secure and reliable messaging for critical transactions. They apply to all types of banks, including national, scheduled, private, government-owned, and multinational corporations (MNCs).

    **Examples**

    - OTP message required for completing a net-banking transaction.
    - OTP message required for completing a credit or debit card transaction at a merchant location.

    | Actual message | Required template format to register on DLT |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | 012345 is your OTP for fund transfer for amount Rs.5,000 to Rahul. OTP is valid for 1 minute. Do not share this OTP with anyone | `{#var#}` is your OTP for fund transfer for amount `{#var#}` to `{#var#}`. OTP is valid for `{#var#}` minute(s). Do not share this OTP with anyone. |
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Service Implicit">
    Service Implicit templates are for messages sent without explicit customer consent. They apply when:

    - Consent can be reasonably inferred from customer behaviour or existing relationships
    - Messages result from customer actions or established sender-recipient interactions
    - Content is non-promotional
    - Blocking such communication isn't in the customer's best interest

    **Examples**

    - Confirmation messages for net-banking and credit or debit card transactions
    - Product purchase confirmation, delivery status, and similar messages from e-commerce websites
    - OTPs required for e-commerce websites, app logins, social media apps, authentication or verification links, securities trading, account operations, KYC, e-wallet registration, and so on
    - Periodic balance info, bill generation, bill dispatch, due date reminders, recharge confirmation (DTH, cable, prepaid electricity recharge, and so on)

    <Accordion title="More examples">
      - Customers making payments through a payment wallet on an e-commerce website or mobile app, where an OTP is sent to complete the transaction
      - Telecom Service Provider (TSP) and Internet Service Provider (ISP) messages
      - Delivery notifications, updates and periodic upgrades
      - Messages from retail stores related to bills and warranty
      - Messages from schools, such as attendance and transport alerts
      - Messages from hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, radiologists and pathologists about registration, appointments, discharge and reports
      - Confirmatory messages from app-based services
      - Government, DoT and TRAI mandated messages
      - Service updates from car workshops, repair shops and gadget service centres
      - Directory services like Justdial and Yellow Pages
      - Day-end or month-end settlement alerts to securities or account holders
    </Accordion>

    | Actual message | Required template format to register on DLT |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | Thank you for using EMI Facility on your HDFC Bank Credit Card ending with 3495. EMI request for Rs. 100000.00 executed on 20/06/2024. | Thank you for using EMI Facility on your HDFC Bank Credit Card ending with `{#var#}`. EMI request for `{#var#}` executed on `{#var#}`. |
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Promotional">
    Promotional templates are used for:

    - Marketing messages and advertisements
    - Content promoting products, goods, or services

    <Warning title="Key points to remember">
      - Messages mixing service and promotional content are usually classified as promotional
      - Promotional content requires explicit customer consent
      - Messages are subject to preference and consent scrubbing before sending
      - Messages cannot be sent without explicit consent for the promotional aspect
    </Warning>

    **Examples**

    - Offer messages to new users, like "Shop for 3999 and get 10% off on our App. Limited time offer. T&C. Download the App now..."
    - Pack upgrade messages to existing customers, like "Upgrade to our pro plan. Get credit limit of 1k and pay once in 30 days. Click here...."

    | Actual message | Required template format to register on DLT |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | Explore our latest Winter Collection Launch. Exclusive 10% discount for you. Valid till 10/07/2024. Use Coupon - XYZABC | Explore our latest Winter Collection Launch. Exclusive `{#var#}` discount for you. Valid till `{#var#}`. Use Coupon - `{#var#}` |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## General template validation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Content rules" defaultOpen>
    - The organization name or brand name must appear in the template.
    - Do not use 2 or more spaces between two words, before a word, or after a word.
    - All special characters are currently allowed.
    - Adding non-English alphanumeric and special characters in a message qualifies it as a Unicode SMS, which has a lower character limit per message than a Text SMS.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Category and header rules">
    - Transactional content templates are only available for banks and digital wallets duly permitted or approved by the RBI.
    - Transactional and Service Implicit templates can be created under alpha headers.
    - Promotional templates can be created under numeric headers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Variable rules">
    - Transactional and Service category messages must have a variable.
    - Promotional category messages can have completely fixed content or include a variable part.
    - The maximum allowed variable length is **30 characters**. Spaces, special characters and regular characters all count as characters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info title="Using another entity name in templates">
If a Principal Entity (PE) uses the name of another entity in their templates, the Telecom Service Provider (TSP) registers it on the presumption that a business relationship exists with that entity, without any accountability to validate it. Valid proofs and justification, if sought pursuant to any complaints by TRAI or a PE, must be furnished by the registering PE.
</Info>

### Character limits

| Number of messages | Text characters | Unicode characters |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| 1 SMS | 160 | 70 |
| 2 SMS | 306 | 134 |
| 3 SMS | 459 | 201 |
| 4 SMS | 612 | 268 |
| 5 SMS | 765 | 335 |

## Best practices

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Do's">
    <Check>
      - Use the Promotional category only for communications sent from a numeric Sender ID.
      - Use the Transactional category only for banking enterprises and for OTP messages.
      - Choose a relevant name for templates.
      - Use the **Text** message type for all general messages and **Unicode** for regional messages.
      - Insert the `{#var#}` variable against values.
    </Check>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Don'ts">
    <Error>
      - Do not skip linking consent templates for Promotional content templates.
      - Do not tag the same content template against multiple headers.
      - Do not select the Transactional category as a non-banking enterprise.
      - Do not use double spaces in templates. You can pre-check this by verifying the template in any text editor before submission.
      - Do not make the whole template a variable. Mention the template content in between the variables, and keep templates at least 6 characters long.
    </Error>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
