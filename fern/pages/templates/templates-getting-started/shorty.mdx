Fyno Shorty turns long links into trackable short URLs. You can generate general URLs or user-specific URLs, so you can track clicks at the notification event, user or channel level.

<Info>
Shortened URLs stay valid for 30 days.
</Info>

## Shorten URLs in your placeholders

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Fyno Shorty">

In the **Template Placeholders** pop-up, click **Configure** on the **Shorten and track URLs with Fyno Shorty** banner.

![](/images/dyncontent-v2-placeholders-form.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the URLs to track">

Fyno Shorty lists the URLs in your placeholders. Turn on the toggle for each URL whose clicks you want to track.

![](/images/shorty_enable_toggle.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the URL type">

Select the type of URL to generate.

| | General | User-Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tracks clicks at** | Notification event level | User and channel level |
| **Recommended for** | Product URLs for Google or Facebook ads, or when you only need the number of clicks | Unique links per user, such as payment links, or when you need the click event at the message level |

![](/images/shorty_configure_type.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Exclude channels (optional)">

Turn on **Exclude Channels** to skip URL shortening for specific channels. For example, if your template has SMS, WhatsApp and Email, and you don't want to shorten URLs in email, select **Email** from the dropdown.

![](/images/shorty_exclude_channels.png)

</Step>
</Steps>

## Create custom URLs

Use a custom URL when you want to build a dynamic URL from several placeholder values and shorten it. For example, you might combine a `paymentURL` placeholder and a UTM source placeholder into one URL.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Custom URL tab">

In the Fyno Shorty pop-up, go to the **Custom URL** tab.

![](/images/shorty_customurl.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Add a URL">

Click **Add URL**. You can create up to 3 custom URLs.

</Step>
<Step title="Build the URL from placeholders">

Add the placeholders that make up the URL, as shown below.

![](/images/shorty_config_updated.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Use the system placeholder in your template">

Fyno shows a system placeholder at the bottom of the pop-up for each custom URL. Use that system placeholder in your template content, instead of the original URL.

</Step>
</Steps>
