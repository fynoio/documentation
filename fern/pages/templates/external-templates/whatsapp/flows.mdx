If you have already integrated any of the below providers and would like to fetch the flows , Fyno can help you with that! We support below WhatsApp service providers.

1. Meta (by Facebook)
2. Meta Direct

In case you do have any of these providers already integrated, then all you will need to do is follow the below steps and sync your templates.

<Steps>
  <Step>Log into your Fyno account and from the left navigation bar, find "**Templates**" and then "**External Templates**".</Step>
  <Step>On the new page that opens, you can go to WhatsApp Flows Tab and sync your flows by clicking on "**Re-sync button**" ![](/images/whatsapp_flows_sync.png)</Step>
  <Step>
    Selecting from the below options:
    <ol>
      <li>Select Channel as WhatsApp</li>
      <li>Select Custom name (of the integration created).</li>
      <li>Click Sync. All the meta templates will be synced to your Fyno workspace now.</li>
    </ol>
    ![](/images/whatsapp_external_template_sync.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>
