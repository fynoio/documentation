Use a Marketing template to send promotions, announcements or campaign notifications to users. Creating one takes four steps:

1. [Create the template](#step-1-create-the-template)
2. [Build the message](#step-2-build-the-message), as a **Common** message, a **Carousel** or a **Calling Permission Request**
3. [Add more languages](#step-3-add-more-languages-optional) (optional)
4. [Save or send for approval](#step-4-save-or-send-for-approval)

## Step 1: Create the template

Navigate to **Templates → External → WhatsApp Templates**, then click **Create**. Fill in the dialog and click **Create Template**.

![](/images/waext-v2-create-dialog.png)

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Template Name** | A unique name in lowercase, with underscores instead of spaces. For example, `summer_sale_offer`. |
| **Integrations** | The WhatsApp Business Account to create the template in. The template can only send messages from this account's phone numbers. |
| **Category** | Select **Marketing**. |
| **Language** | One or more languages. You can add or remove languages later. |

## Step 2: Build the message

In **Template Type**, choose the type of message:

| Template type | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Common** | Send a standard message with a header, body, footer and buttons. |
| **Carousel** | Send up to 10 swipeable cards, each with its own media and buttons. |
| **Calling Permission Request** | Ask users for permission to call them on WhatsApp. |

The header appears at the top of the message. Choose one type.

Click **Choose JPG or PNG file** and upload a sample image (maximum 5 MB).

Click **Choose MP4 file** and upload a sample video (maximum 16 MB).

![](/images/waext-v2-header-video.png)

Click **Choose PDF file** and upload a sample document (maximum 100 MB).

![](/images/waext-v2-header-document.png)

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Location">

Shows a location, with its name and address, above the message body.

![](/images/waext-v2-header-location.png)

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
Media samples are only used by Meta to review your template. Do not include any customer information.
</Note>

</Step>
<Step title="Write the body">

Enter the main message (maximum 1,032 characters). You can format text and add variables. Provide a sample value for every variable so Meta can review the message.

![](/images/sample-for-body-content-marketing.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Add a footer (optional)">

A short line at the bottom of the message, such as a disclaimer or opt-out instructions. Maximum 60 characters, with no variables.

![](/images/sample-footer-marketing.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Set the message validity period">

Choose how long Meta tries to deliver the message, for example 30 days. If it isn't delivered in time, you aren't charged and the recipient doesn't see it.

![](/images/message-validity-period.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Add buttons (optional)">

Click **Add Button** and choose a type. The menu shows how many of each type you can add.

![](/images/waext-v2-cta-visit-website.png)

<Accordion title="Call to Action: Copy Coupon Code">

![](/images/copy-coupon-code.png)

<Tab title="Carousel">

A swipeable message with up to **10 cards**, shown below a message body. Each card has its own text, media and buttons. Use it for product catalogs, multiple offers, event listings or booking options.

<Step title="Write the body">

<Step title="Write the card content">

Enter the caption for the card in **Card Content**. You can add variables, and you must provide sample values for them.

</Step>
<Step title="Add the card media">

Every card needs media. In **Media Type**, choose **Image** or **Video** and upload a sample. The sample is only used for Meta's review, so don't include any customer information.

<Accordion title="Image">

Click **Choose JPG or PNG file** and upload a sample image.

![](/images/waext-v2-carousel-sample-image.png)

<Accordion title="Video">

Click **Choose MP4 file** and upload a sample video.

![](/images/waext-v2-carousel-sample-video.png)

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

</Step>
<Step title="Add card buttons">

Buttons are set per card, with up to **2 buttons per card**. Click **Add Button** and choose a type.

![](/images/quick-reply-button.png)

Use a **static URL** to send the same link to everyone, or select **Dynamic URL** to send a different link to each user. Select **Track URL** to track clicks.

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Step title="Add a header (optional)">

Choose **None**, or **Text** and enter a short title in **Header Content** (maximum 60 characters).

<Step title="Write the body">

Enter the message shown above the permission card (maximum 1,032 characters). You can add variables.

</Step>
<Step title="Add a footer (optional)">

Maximum 60 characters, with no variables.

Choose how long Meta tries to deliver the message, for example 30 days.

</Note>

## Step 3: Add more languages (optional)

To reach users in different regions, click **Add** next to **Languages** and add a version of the template in each language. Translate the message and button text, and keep the same variables in every language.

## Step 4: Save or send for approval

When your template is ready, save it as a draft or send it to Meta for approval. See [Save as Draft vs Send for Approval](./save-or-send-for-approval).
