If a template already exists in your WhatsApp Business Account, sync it into Fyno and use it right away, without recreating it.

Syncing is available for these WhatsApp providers:

- Kaleyra
- Meta
- Meta Direct
- ValueFirst
- Gupshup Enterprise

## What syncing imports

| Data | What's included |
| --- | --- |
| **Message templates** | All templates, whatever their status, such as Approved, Pending, Rejected or Disabled. |
| **WhatsApp Flows** | All flows in the account. |
| **Template metadata** | Category, quality rating and status updates. |

## Sync all templates

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Sync WhatsApp Templates">

On the **External Templates** page, open **Sync WhatsApp Templates**.

</Step>
<Step title="Select the integration">

In **Integrations**, select the integration to sync from. Integrations are grouped by WABA / Namespace.

![](/images/waext-v2-sync-dialog.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Click Sync">

Fyno imports all the templates and flows in the selected integration, whatever their approval status.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Re-sync a single template

Re-sync one template when you only need its latest details, instead of syncing everything. Re-sync a template when:

- Its status has changed, such as to Approved, Rejected, Disabled or Pending.
- Its content or components have been updated.
- You've fixed and resubmitted a rejected template.
- A WhatsApp Flow it uses has been created or changed.

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the template">

On the **External Templates** page, find the template in the list.

</Step>
<Step title="Click the Re-sync icon">

Click the **Re-sync** icon on the template's row. Fyno fetches the latest version and status of the template.

![](/images/waext-v2-resync-icon.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Check the last synced time">

The time next to the icon, such as **2M ago**, shows when the template was last synced. Check it to confirm the template was updated.

</Step>
</Steps>
