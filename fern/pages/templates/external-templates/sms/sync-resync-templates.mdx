Sync your DLT templates into Fyno to keep them up to date with the DLT platform. Syncing lets you:

- Import templates from the DLT platform.
- Refresh their approval status.
- Fetch newly approved or rejected templates.

## Sync SMS templates

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Sync SMS Templates">

Navigate to **Templates → External → SMS**, then click **Sync**.

</Step>
<Step title="Select the DLT integration">

In **DLT Integration**, select the DLT integration to sync the templates from.

![](/images/smsext-v2-sync-dialog.png)

</Step>
<Step title="Upload the CSV file">

Click **Upload .CSV File** and upload the CSV file you downloaded from the DLT platform.

<Warning>
Upload the file exactly as you downloaded it. Don't make any changes to it.
</Warning>

</Step>
<Step title="Click Sync">

All the SMS templates in the CSV file are synced to your Fyno workspace, so Fyno reflects their latest state on the DLT platform.

</Step>
</Steps>
